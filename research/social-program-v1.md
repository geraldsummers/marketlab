# Prospective social and news program v1

Status: superseded before D0 because the required local Farcaster Snapchain
node was unavailable. No v1 observation was admitted to a training,
development, or holdout period. Its machine-readable preregistration is
preserved in `social-program-v1.lock.json`.

V1 required Bluesky Jetstream, Nostr public relays, a locally operated
Farcaster Snapchain read node, GDELT GKG, reviewed crypto RSS feeds, and the
Hyperliquid point-in-time universe. Its schedule, feature policy, five theory
tests, Holm family, execution assumptions, and trading restrictions were
otherwise the same as v2.

The v1 program clock remained in `WAITING_FOR_REQUIRED_SOURCES`. It was
superseded rather than silently weakening its frozen source requirement.
